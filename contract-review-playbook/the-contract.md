# MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription and Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is
entered into as of September 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and
between **Corvantic Analytics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability
company with its principal place of business at 1210 Barrow Creek
Parkway, Suite 400, Austin, Texas 78744 ("Provider"), and **Meridian
Fabrication Group, Inc.**, an Ohio corporation with its principal
place of business at 4400 Runyon Avenue, Dayton, Ohio 45404
("Customer"). Provider and Customer are each a "party" and together
the "parties."

**RECITALS.** Provider operates a cloud-based industrial analytics
platform. Customer operates precision metal fabrication facilities and
wishes to subscribe to the platform to analyze production, quality,
maintenance, and supplier data. The parties therefore agree as
follows.

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 "Affiliate"** means an entity that controls, is controlled by,
or is under common control with a party, where control means ownership
of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests.

**1.2 "Aggregated Data"** means Customer Data that has been combined
with other data or modified so that the result does not identify
Customer or any User as its source.

**1.3 "Customer Data"** means electronic data submitted to the
Platform by or on behalf of Customer, including production, machine,
sensor, quality, scrap, maintenance, scheduling, supplier, and cost
data, and files and records uploaded by Users.

**1.4 "Customer Indemnitees"** means Customer, its Affiliates, and
their respective officers, directors, employees, and agents.

**1.5 "Customer Systems"** means Customer's information technology
systems, networks, devices, historians, sensors, and software,
including third party systems operated for Customer, other than the
Platform.

**1.6 "Documentation"** means Provider's then-current user guides and
technical documentation for the Platform made available through the
Support Portal.

**1.7 "Facility"** means a Customer manufacturing location identified
in an Order Form.

**1.8 "Fees"** means all amounts payable by Customer under this
Agreement, including Subscription Fees, fees at the Overage Rate, fees
for Professional Services, and one time fees.

**1.9 "Initial Term"** has the meaning given in Section 18.1.

**1.10 "Order Form"** means an ordering document for the Platform or
Professional Services that is signed by both parties and references
this Agreement.

**1.11 "Overage Rate"** means the per-unit rate stated in an Order
Form for usage exceeding the quantities included in the Subscription
Fees.

**1.12 "Platform"** means Provider's proprietary cloud-based
industrial analytics software platform, including the modules
identified in an Order Form, and all updates to it made generally
available by Provider.

**1.13 "Professional Services"** means implementation, configuration,
integration, training, or advisory services described in an Order Form
or statement of work.

**1.14 "Renewal Term"** has the meaning given in Section 18.2.

**1.15 "Security Incident"** means actual or reasonably suspected
unauthorized access to, or unauthorized acquisition, use, disclosure,
alteration, or destruction of, Customer Data in Provider's possession
or control.

**1.16 "Services"** means the Platform, Support, and Professional
Services.

**1.17 "Subscription Fees"** means the recurring fees for Platform
subscriptions stated in an Order Form, exclusive of fees at the
Overage Rate, fees for Professional Services, and one time fees.

**1.18 "Subscription Term"** means the Initial Term together with all
Renewal Terms.

**1.19 "Support"** means the support services described in Section 14.

**1.20 "Support Portal"** means Provider's online support and
documentation site for subscribing customers.

**1.21 "Usage Data"** means technical logs, telemetry, and metadata
generated by the Platform's operation, such as feature usage, query
volumes, session information, and performance metrics, excluding the
content of Customer Data.

**1.22 "Users"** means individuals authorized by Customer to use the
Platform, limited to employees and contractors of Customer and its
Affiliates acting for Customer's benefit.

## 2. The Platform; Access Rights

**2.1 Access grant.** Subject to this Agreement and the applicable
Order Form, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive,
non-transferable (except as permitted under Section 29) right during
the Subscription Term for Users to access and use the Platform for the
subscribed Facilities, for Customer's internal business purposes.

**2.2 Users.** Customer is responsible for Users' compliance with this
Agreement and for maintaining the confidentiality of User credentials.
Customer will notify Provider promptly of any known unauthorized use
of a User account.

**2.3 Documentation license.** Customer may reproduce Documentation
internally as reasonably needed to use the Platform.

**2.4 Condition.** The rights granted in this Section 2 are
conditioned on Customer's timely payment of all Fees.

## 3. Order Forms; Order of Precedence

**3.1 Order Forms.** Each Order Form is effective when signed by both
parties and is governed by this Agreement.

**3.2 Precedence.** If an Order Form conflicts with the body of this

the body of this Agreement, the Schedule controls with respect to its

**4.2 Statements of work.** Additional Professional Services will be

**4.3 Cooperation.** Provider's performance dates depend on Customer's
timely performance of its responsibilities and are extended day for

## 5. Customer Responsibilities; Use Restrictions

**20.4 Survival.** Sections 1, 7, 8.2, 9, 15 (as to accrued amounts),

# SCHEDULE A: INFORMATION SECURITY SCHEDULE

This Schedule A describes Provider's information security program for
the Platform and forms part of the Agreement.

**A-1. Program.** Provider maintains a written information security
program aligned to a recognized industry framework, reviewed at least
annually, with a designated security officer accountable for it.

**A-2. Access controls.** Production access is limited to authorized
personnel with a need to know, protected by unique credentials and
multi-factor authentication, reviewed quarterly, and revoked within
one (1) business day of role change or separation.

**A-3. Encryption.** Customer Data is encrypted in transit using TLS
1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent standard.
Encryption keys are managed under documented key management
procedures.

**A-4. Audits and testing.** Provider undergoes an annual SOC 2 Type
II examination covering the Platform and will provide the current
report on request under confidentiality. Provider performs annual
third party penetration testing and remediates critical findings
promptly.

**A-5. Subprocessors.** Provider hosts the Platform with a nationally
recognized cloud infrastructure provider in data centers located in
the continental United States and maintains a list of subprocessors
available on the Support Portal. Provider remains responsible for
subprocessors' performance.

**A-6. Incident response.** Provider maintains a documented incident
response plan, tests it at least annually, and follows it in
responding to Security Incidents, including the notice obligations in
Section 11 of the Agreement.

**A-7. Business continuity.** Provider maintains backup and disaster
recovery procedures with a recovery point objective of twenty-four
(24) hours and a recovery time objective of forty-eight (48) hours for
the Platform.

**A-8. Personnel.** Provider performs background checks on personnel
with production access as permitted by law and trains personnel on
security and confidentiality at hire and annually.

**A-9. Shared responsibility; limitation.** The security of the
Services is a shared responsibility. Notwithstanding anything to the
contrary in this Agreement, including Sections 11 and 23 of the
