v.

PLAINTIFF'S FIRST SET OF INTERROGATORIES AND REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION OF DOCUMENTS TO DEFENDANT

Pursuant to Rules 33 and 34 of the Tennessee Rules of Civil Procedure, Plaintiff Marlene Whitfield propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents to Defendant Cumberland Ridge Logistics, LLC, to be answered fully, in writing, and under oath. These requests were served on July 20, 2026. By agreement of counsel, responses are due on or before August 31, 2026.

DEFINITIONS

As used herein, "Company," "you," and "your" mean Defendant Cumberland Ridge Logistics, LLC, together with its officers, employees, agents, and anyone acting on its behalf. "Document" has the broadest meaning permitted under Rule 34 and includes electronically stored information, email, text messages, notes, and drafts. "Identify," when used with respect to a person, means to state the person's full name, job title, and last known contact information. "Plaintiff" means Marlene Whitfield.

INTERROGATORIES

INTERROGATORY NO. 1: Identify each person who supplied information used in answering these interrogatories, and state which answers each person supplied information for.

INTERROGATORY NO. 2: Describe the Company's management and reporting structure for its operations department from January 1, 2025 to the present, including each person who held the title of operations manager or an equivalent position.

INTERROGATORY NO. 3: Identify each person who participated in, was consulted regarding, or approved the decision to terminate Plaintiff's employment, and describe each such person's role in that decision.

INTERROGATORY NO. 4: Describe Plaintiff's performance history during her employment, including each performance evaluation, rating, counseling session, or disciplinary action, the date of each, and the person who administered it.

INTERROGATORY NO. 5: Describe each complaint or concern Plaintiff raised regarding driver hours of service, driver logs, or dispatch scheduling practices, and describe in detail every action the Company took in response to each.

INTERROGATORY NO. 6: Describe the corporate restructuring referenced in the Company's April 6, 2026 memorandum to Plaintiff, including who first proposed it, when it was proposed, the business justification for it, each position affected by it, and when each decision concerning it was made.

INTERROGATORY NO. 7: State the reason for the termination of Plaintiff's employment.

INTERROGATORY NO. 8: Identify each person who held an operations manager position at the Company between January 1, 2025 and the present, state each such person's date of hire and most recent performance rating, and describe the criteria used to determine which position or person would be retained in any restructuring.

INTERROGATORY NO. 9: Identify each person with knowledge of the Company's driver hours of service practices, dispatch scheduling practices, or electronic logging device records for the period October 1, 2025 through April 30, 2026.

INTERROGATORY NO. 10: Describe the Company's document retention policies and each step the Company has taken to preserve documents relating to Plaintiff or her claims since February 3, 2026, including the issuance of any litigation hold.

INTERROGATORY NO. 11: Identify each insurance policy that does or may provide coverage for the claims asserted in this action, including the insurer, policy number, policy period, and limits of coverage.

INTERROGATORY NO. 12: Identify each person the Company expects to call as a witness at the trial of this matter, and each person known to the Company to have knowledge of the facts alleged in the Complaint or of any defense raised in the Answer, with a brief description of each person's knowledge.

REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION

REQUEST NO. 1: All documents identified in, relied upon, or consulted in preparing the answers to the foregoing interrogatories.

REQUEST NO. 2: All employee handbooks, personnel policies, disciplinary policies, and reduction in force policies in effect at any time from January 1, 2024 to the present.

REQUEST NO. 3: All documents and communications concerning any complaint or concern raised by Plaintiff regarding driver hours of service, driver logs, or dispatch practices, including all documents concerning any investigation of or response to such complaint.

REQUEST NO. 4: All documents concerning the decision to terminate Plaintiff.

REQUEST NO. 5: All documents concerning the corporate restructuring described in the April 6, 2026 memorandum to Plaintiff, including all proposals, financial analyses, meeting agendas, meeting notes, and communications.

REQUEST NO. 6: All documents concerning any severance, separation, or settlement offer made to Plaintiff.

REQUEST NO. 7: All performance evaluations of Plaintiff, and all performance evaluations of each other person who held an operations manager position at the Company from January 1, 2024 to the present.

REQUEST NO. 8: All documents concerning driver hours of service compliance for the period October 1, 2025 through April 30, 2026, including dispatch scheduling communications for January 2026, electronic logging device exception reports, and any related audits.

REQUEST NO. 9: Plaintiff's complete personnel file.

REQUEST NO. 10: All insurance policies identified in response to Interrogatory No. 11.
