1. Separation. The parties acknowledge that Employee's position, Operations Manager, East, was eliminated in a corporate restructuring and that her employment ended effective April 17, 2026.

4. General release. Employee releases the Company and its officers, employees, and agents from all claims arising out of her employment or the end of her employment, including claims under federal, state, and local law, to the fullest extent permitted by law. Nothing in this Agreement prevents Employee from filing a charge with a government agency or limits any claim that cannot lawfully be waived.

5. No admission. This Agreement is not an admission of liability or wrongdoing by either party.

6. Non-disparagement. Each party agrees not to make disparaging statements about the other. Nothing herein restricts truthful testimony under oath or communications with government agencies.

7. Reference. The Company will respond to reference inquiries with dates of employment, final title, and confirmation that the position was eliminated in a restructuring.

8. Consideration period. Employee has twenty one (21) days from receipt to consider this offer and is advised to consult an attorney. The offer lapses if not accepted within that period.
