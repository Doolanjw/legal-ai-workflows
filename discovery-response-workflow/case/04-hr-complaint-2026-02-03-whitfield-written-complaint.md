CUMBERLAND RIDGE LOGISTICS, LLC
EMPLOYEE COMPLAINT (SUBMITTED IN WRITING)

To: Renee Paulk, HR Director
From: Marlene Whitfield, Operations Manager, East
Date: February 3, 2026
Subject: Formal complaint regarding driver hours of service and dispatch practices

Renee,

I am submitting this complaint in writing because I raised the same issue with Dale Kestner on January 12 and I do not believe it was taken seriously.

The issue is this. Dispatch has been pressuring drivers to meet delivery windows that cannot be met within federal hours of service limits, and the pressure carries an implied instruction to make the logs work. The clearest example is the January 8 email from dispatcher Cody Brantley to driver Ray Mabry about a Memphis delivery. Ray was near the end of his 14 hour window. The email told him the customer window could not move, to handle it however he needed to, and that the log notes would be sorted out the following Monday. Since late November at least two other drivers have described similar pressure to me in person.

If drivers are exceeding their driving or on duty limits, or making log entries that do not reflect actual duty status, the company is violating federal motor carrier safety regulations and our own DOT compliance policy. This is a safety issue before it is anything else.

When I raised this with Dale on January 12 his written response the next day told me not to raise it with drivers directly and said a meeting with Safety was not necessary. I disagree. I am requesting that HR open a formal review, that Safety and Compliance audit the electronic logging device records and dispatch communications for December and January, and that dispatch scheduling practices be corrected so drivers are not put in this position.

I am available to discuss any of this at any time. I am raising it because it is my job to raise it.

Marlene Whitfield
Operations Manager, East
