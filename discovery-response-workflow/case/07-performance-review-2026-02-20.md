CUMBERLAND RIDGE LOGISTICS, LLC
MID-CYCLE PERFORMANCE REVIEW

Employee: Marlene Whitfield
Title: Operations Manager, East
Reviewer: Dale Kestner, VP of Operations
Review Date: February 20, 2026
Review Period: November 2025 through February 2026

OVERALL RATING: 3 of 5 (Meets Expectations)

Summary. This mid-cycle check-in reflects a mixed quarter. The East region's operational numbers remain solid. On time delivery for the period was 92.8 percent, slightly under the 93 percent goal but within normal winter variance. Driver retention remains strong and there were no preventable accidents in the period.

Areas of concern. The principal concern this period is communication friction with dispatch. Working relationships between the East operations desk and the dispatch team have deteriorated since December. Dispatch personnel report feeling second guessed and monitored, and cross department meetings that used to be routine have become tense. As noted in the November review, Marlene's directness is an asset with drivers and customers but is creating drag internally. A manager at her level is expected to resolve interdepartmental disagreements through the appropriate channels and to maintain productive working relationships even where she disagrees with a peer group's approach.

Strengths. Customer relationships remain excellent. The weekend rotation cross training goal from November is complete.

Expectations going forward. Restore a normal working cadence with dispatch by end of Q2. Route any operational disagreements with dispatch through the VP of Operations rather than working them laterally. Continue current on time and safety performance.
