CUMBERLAND RIDGE LOGISTICS, LLC
MEMORANDUM

To: Marlene Whitfield
From: Dale Kestner, VP of Operations
Date: April 6, 2026
Re: Elimination of position

Marlene,

This memo confirms what we discussed in our meeting today.

As part of a corporate restructuring, the company is consolidating its two regional operations manager positions into a single company wide operations manager role. As a result, your position, Operations Manager, East, is being eliminated. Your employment with Cumberland Ridge Logistics, LLC will end effective April 17, 2026.

This restructuring reflects business conditions, including flat equipment counts, revenue tracking under plan, and increasing overlap between the two regions. The decision to eliminate the position was reached following the restructuring review conducted on March 30, 2026, at which the company's financial analysis and documented selection criteria were considered. The selection between the two operations manager positions was based on objective factors including comparative regional consolidation requirements and recent performance assessments.

You will be paid through April 17, 2026, together with all accrued and unused paid time off, in accordance with company policy. Information regarding your benefits, including continuation coverage, is included in the packet provided by Human Resources today. The company anticipates presenting you with a separation agreement, including a severance offer, under separate cover.

Please direct any questions regarding pay or benefits to Renee Paulk in Human Resources. The company thanks you for your seven years of service.

Dale Kestner
VP of Operations
(signed, April 6, 2026)
