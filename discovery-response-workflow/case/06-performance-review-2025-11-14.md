CUMBERLAND RIDGE LOGISTICS, LLC
ANNUAL PERFORMANCE REVIEW

Employee: Marlene Whitfield
Title: Operations Manager, East
Hire Date: March 11, 2019
Reviewer: Dale Kestner, VP of Operations
Review Date: November 14, 2025
Review Period: November 2024 through October 2025

OVERALL RATING: 4 of 5 (Exceeds Expectations)

Summary. Marlene had a strong year. The East region posted a 94.2 percent on time delivery rate, the best of our two regions, while absorbing the loss of two senior drivers in the spring. Her driver retention numbers continue to lead the company. She rebuilt the weekend coverage rotation after the Chattanooga lane expansion and did it without adding headcount.

Strengths. Marlene knows the freight, knows the drivers, and knows the customers. She is the manager drivers call first when something goes wrong on the road, and that trust shows up in our retention and safety numbers. Her region recorded zero preventable accidents this review period. Her weekly operations reports are consistently the most useful documents I receive.

Development areas. Marlene can be direct to the point of bluntness in cross department settings, particularly with dispatch and billing. Nothing rising to a concern, but as the company grows she will need to bring people along rather than simply being right. I would also like to see her delegate more of the routine customer calls to her lead coordinators.

Goals for next period. Maintain on time performance above 93 percent. Cross train a backup for the weekend rotation. Participate in the Q1 planning cycle for the proposed Tri-Cities lane.

Rating history: 2022 review 4 of 5. 2023 review 4 of 5. 2024 review 4 of 5.
