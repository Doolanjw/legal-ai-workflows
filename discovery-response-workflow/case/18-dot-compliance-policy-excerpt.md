CUMBERLAND RIDGE LOGISTICS, LLC
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK, SECTION 9 (EXCERPT)
DOT HOURS OF SERVICE AND ELECTRONIC LOGGING COMPLIANCE
Effective June 1, 2024

9.1 Policy. The Company operates in full compliance with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations, including the hours of service rules in 49 C.F.R. Part 395. Safety is the first consideration in every dispatch, scheduling, and delivery decision. No load, customer commitment, or delivery window justifies a violation of hours of service limits.

9.2 Limits. Property carrying drivers may drive a maximum of 11 hours after 10 consecutive hours off duty, may not drive beyond the 14th consecutive hour after coming on duty, and may not drive after 60 hours on duty in 7 consecutive days or 70 hours in 8 consecutive days, as applicable. Required breaks and off duty periods must be observed as provided in the regulations.

9.3 Electronic logging. All drivers record duty status through the Company's electronic logging device system. Drivers must certify their own records. Log edits are permitted only to correct genuine errors, must be annotated with the reason, and require the driver's recertification. No employee may edit, direct the editing of, or request the editing of a driver's log to conceal a violation or to make a run appear compliant.

9.4 Scheduling. Dispatch personnel must plan loads within available driver hours. If a delivery window cannot be met within a driver's available hours, dispatch must reschedule the delivery, reassign the load, or notify the customer. Pressuring a driver to exceed available hours is prohibited and is grounds for discipline up to termination.

9.5 Reporting. Any employee who believes hours of service rules or this policy are being violated must report the concern to the Safety and Compliance Manager, the HR Director, or any member of management. The Company prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports a safety or compliance concern in good faith.
