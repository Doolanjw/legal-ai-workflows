CUMBERLAND RIDGE LOGISTICS, LLC
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Dale Kestner, VP of Operations; Renee Paulk, HR Director
From: Lena Ostrander, CFO
Date: March 25, 2026
Re: Proposed consolidation of operations management structure

This memo summarizes the financial case for consolidating our two regional operations manager positions into a single company wide operations manager role, and proposes a review meeting to consider it.

Background. Since the 2023 reorganization we have run operations under two regional managers, East and West, each reporting to the VP of Operations. That structure made sense when the Chattanooga lane expansion was projected to add three to five new dedicated customer accounts per year. Actual growth has come in well under projection. Combined tractor count is flat year over year, and the two regions increasingly share lanes, drivers, and customers, which produces duplicated planning work and split accountability.

Financial case. Eliminating one operations manager position saves approximately 128,000 dollars annually in salary, benefits, and bonus. Q1 revenue is tracking 6 percent under plan and fuel costs are running above the hedged assumption. Corporate overhead is the category with the most room, and this is the largest single overhead consolidation available to us that does not touch driver pay or maintenance.

Proposal. Consolidate to a single operations manager covering both regions, reporting to the VP of Operations, with the two lead coordinators absorbing routine regional scheduling. This memo does not propose which position or person would be affected. That selection, if the consolidation is approved, should be made on documented, objective criteria at the review meeting.

Next step. I propose we meet on March 30 with HR to review this analysis and decide whether to proceed. My office will circulate the supporting financial detail before the meeting.

Lena Ostrander
Chief Financial Officer
