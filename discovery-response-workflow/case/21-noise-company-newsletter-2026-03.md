THE RIDGE REPORT
Cumberland Ridge Logistics Employee Newsletter, March 2026

SAFETY MILESTONE. Congratulations to our entire driver fleet on reaching 1.2 million consecutive miles without a preventable accident as of February 15. That is the longest streak in company history. Keep it rolling, safely.

DRIVER OF THE QUARTER. Hats off to Luis Carbajal, who talked a stranded motorist through a tire change on I 75 near Caryville in January and still made his delivery window. The customer wrote us a letter. Luis gets the reserved parking spot and a 250 dollar gas card.

SHOP CORNER. The maintenance team reminds everyone that spring inspection season starts April 1. Drivers, get your trucks scheduled with the shop early, the calendar fills up fast and nobody likes a Friday afternoon inspection.

BENEFITS REMINDER. Open enrollment materials for the 2026 to 2027 plan year go out in May. Watch your mailbox. Questions go to Amy Sturdivant in HR.

POTLUCK. The spring potluck is Friday, April 24 at the Sutherland Avenue yard, weather permitting. The shop is defending its chili title. Sign up sheet is in the break room. Last year we ran out of forks, so if you can bring forks, bring forks.

BIRTHDAYS AND ANNIVERSARIES. March birthdays: Pete Yancey, Denise Calloway, Luis Carbajal, and eight of our drivers, see the break room board. Work anniversaries: Ed Kowalcik marks 12 years with the company this month. Thank you Ed.
