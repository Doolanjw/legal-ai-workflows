## CRL-0008: Paulk and Aldana email thread, March 18 to 19, 2026

**File:** 08-email-thread-2026-03-18-19-paulk-aldana-PRIVILEGED.md
**Classification: PRIVILEGED (both communications; withhold the document in full; two log entries drafted below)**

Two communications in the thread, each tested separately.

**Communication 1: Paulk to Aldana, March 18, 2026, 4:51 PM.**

1. Lawyer a participant, and in what capacity: yes. Aldana is the sole recipient, addressed as General Counsel in his legal capacity; the message opens by asking for his legal advice.
2. Legal advice sought or given in the communication itself: yes. The message expressly requests legal advice and a legal risk assessment concerning a contemplated personnel decision.
3. Kept within the circle: yes. One sender, one recipient (counsel). The sender states she has shared her concerns with no one else and will keep the response confidential. Marked PRIVILEGED AND CONFIDENTIAL.
4. Severable business purpose: no. The communication exists to obtain legal advice; the business facts it references are the subject of the advice request, not a severable business communication.

All four prongs cleanly met. PRIVILEGED.

**Communication 2: Aldana to Paulk, March 19, 2026, 11:38 AM.**

1. Lawyer a participant, and in what capacity: yes. Aldana is the author, signing as General Counsel, responding in his legal capacity.
2. Legal advice sought or given in the communication itself: yes. The message states on its face that it responds to a request for legal advice and then gives that advice. The advice content is not restated here.
3. Kept within the circle: yes. Sent only to the original requester, marked PRIVILEGED AND CONFIDENTIAL, ATTORNEY CLIENT COMMUNICATION, with an express instruction not to forward. Nothing in the collected set shows forwarding beyond the circle.
4. Severable business purpose: no. The communication is legal advice throughout.

All four prongs cleanly met. PRIVILEGED.

**Disposition:** Withhold CRL-0008 in its entirety. Log entries 1 and 2 below. Note for Stage 4: the document was mapped at triage to ROG No. 3, ROG No. 6, RFP No. 4, RFP No. 5, and RFP No. 6; responsiveness never overrides privilege, and the withholding must be reflected in the responses and on the served privilege log.

## CRL-0010: HR running notes, Paulk working file, February 3 to May 27, 2026

**File:** 10-hr-notes-file-paulk.md
**Classification: entry level review. One entry PRIVILEGED in part (redaction, logged), two entries ROUTE TO HUMAN REVIEW, ten entries NOT PRIVILEGED.**

Thirteen dated entries. Entries differ, so classification is at the entry level per the protocol.

**Entries 2026-02-03, 2026-02-04, 2026-02-09, 2026-02-13, 2026-02-19, 2026-02-24, 2026-03-26, 2026-03-30, 2026-04-06, 2026-04-17: NOT PRIVILEGED.** No lawyer is a participant in or subject of any of these entries; they record complaint intake, investigation steps, the restructuring decision, the termination meeting, and separation logistics. No colorable basis.

**Entry 2026-03-18: ROUTE TO HUMAN REVIEW.** The entry records that the author consulted the General Counsel regarding the restructuring proposal and related personnel risk, and cross references separate privileged correspondence not kept in this file. Uncertain prong: prong 2 (advice sought or given in the communication itself). The bare fact that counsel was consulted, and the date, are ordinarily discoverable and belong on a privilege log rather than in a withholding; but the entry's description of the consultation's subject may cross from the fact of consultation into revealing the substance of the advice sought. Whether to produce the entry as written, redact the subject matter phrase, or withhold the entry is a judgment call for counsel. The underlying correspondence itself is CRL-0008, classified PRIVILEGED above; nothing in this routing clears that document.

**Entry 2026-05-08: ROUTE TO HUMAN REVIEW.** The entry records that the severance offer draft was finalized and mailed following the General Counsel's review. Uncertain prong: prong 2. The phrase records the fact of attorney review of a document later transmitted to the Plaintiff. The fact of review is likely not itself privileged, but whether the phrase discloses the substance of counsel's input on the draft, or only that review occurred, is not clean on the face of the entry. Counsel decides between producing as written and redacting the attorney review reference.

**Entry 2026-05-27: PRIVILEGED IN PART (redact; log entry 3 below).** The entry contains two distinct components. The first two sentences record business facts: no response to the severance offer, and the lapse date of the consideration window. No lawyer participates in those facts; they are NOT PRIVILEGED. The final sentence, beginning "GA advised," records specific legal advice given by the General Counsel concerning further contact with the offeree. Tested as a communication: (1) the General Counsel is the source of the recorded statement, acting in his legal capacity; (2) legal advice was given and the sentence memorializes it; (3) the record sits in the HR Director's internal working file and was not shown to have left the circle; (4) the business facts in the entry are severable and are produced, so the withholding is confined to the advice. The advice sentence is PRIVILEGED and is redacted, not quoted here beyond its opening words needed to locate it.

**Overall document disposition:** Produce CRL-0010 with (a) the final sentence of the 2026-05-27 entry redacted as privileged, per log entry 3, and (b) entries 2026-03-18 and 2026-05-08 held for the human review determinations above before the production copy is finalized. All other entries produce unredacted, subject to the Stage 4 responsiveness calls.

## CRL-0011: Restructuring implementation thread, March 31, 2026, counsel copied

**File:** 11-email-thread-2026-03-31-restructuring-logistics-CC-aldana.md
**Classification: ROUTE TO HUMAN REVIEW (all three communications; same posture)**

Three communications (Ostrander 9:31 AM; Kestner 11:52 AM; Paulk 2:14 PM), each with the same structure: sent among three business principals with the General Counsel copied on each message.

Tested per communication, the posture is identical for all three:

1. Lawyer a participant: present on each message, but only as a Cc. The originating message states counsel is copied for visibility on timing. No message is addressed to him and he sends nothing in the thread.
2. Legal advice sought or given in the communication itself: not on the face of any message. The visible content is implementation logistics: meeting scheduling, packet preparation, payroll, transition of duties, announcement timing.
3. Kept within the circle: the participants are senior managers plus counsel; no external distribution appears.
4. Severable business purpose: the entire visible content is business execution.

**Uncertain prong and why:** prong 2. On its face no message seeks or gives legal advice, which points toward production. But the protocol expressly routes the lawyer merely copied pattern rather than clearing it, and there is a genuine question a human must weigh: the thread implements the March 30 review outcome, and counsel's presence on timing follows the sequence and communication controls recommended in the privileged March 19 advice (CRL-0008). Whether these messages are ordinary business communications on which counsel is a bystander, or communications made in part to keep counsel informed for the purpose of ongoing legal advice, is exactly the call the gate does not make. Per the design rule, the gate over-refers; it never over-clears.

**Disposition:** Route the entire document. If counsel clears it, it produces against ROG No. 2, ROG No. 3, ROG No. 6, RFP No. 4, and RFP No. 5 per the Stage 2 mapping.

## CRL-0013: Separation agreement and severance offer draft, May 8, 2026

**File:** 13-severance-offer-draft-2026-05-08.md
**Classification: ROUTE TO HUMAN REVIEW**

Two components, tested separately.

**The agreement text (sections 1 through 8 and the offer summary):** transmitted by mail to the Plaintiff on May 8, 2026. Prong 3 fails as to her copy; a document sent to the adverse party is not a confidential attorney client communication, whoever drafted or reviewed it. Standing alone this component would be NOT PRIVILEGED.

**The internal routing note:** the collected copy carries an internal note recording that the General Counsel reviewed the document prior to transmittal and directing filing. That note was not part of what was mailed; it is an internal annotation naming counsel.

**Uncertain prong and why:** prong 2, as to the routing note only. The note records the fact of attorney review of a draft later sent out. The fact of review is ordinarily not privileged, but whether producing the annotated copy discloses counsel's role and input on the release language (the subject counsel specifically flagged in the privileged March 19 communication) is not clean, and a work product question about internal annotations on draft agreements rides with it. Because the classification unit here is the document as collected, and one component is not cleanly clearable, the document routes rather than clears.

**Disposition:** Route. The expected human outcome is production of the agreement text with a decision on redacting the routing note, but that decision belongs to counsel, not the gate.
