# Objection Postures, PROPOSED for counsel's ruling (2026-08-25)

Whitfield v. Cumberland Ridge Logistics, LLC. RULED: approved as
drafted by Jonathan 2026-08-25. These postures govern Stage 4. Postures use three values:
ANSWER (no objection), ANSWER SUBJECT TO OBJECTION (objection stated,
answer given), OBJECTION WITH LIMITED ANSWER (objection leads, answer
narrowed to the proper scope). Production postures use PRODUCE,
PRODUCE SUBJECT TO (stated condition), or WITHHOLD AND LOG.

## Interrogatories

1. Preparers of answers: ANSWER.
2. Operations reporting structure since Jan 2025: ANSWER.
3. Persons involved in the termination decision: ANSWER SUBJECT TO
   OBJECTION. The fact that counsel was consulted is answerable; the
   substance of counsel's involvement is privileged and stays out.
4. Complete performance history: ANSWER SUBJECT TO OBJECTION.
   Adopt the narrower reading flagged at intake: documented
   evaluations, counseling, and discipline; object to "including" as
   illustrative overbreadth.
5. Complaints or concerns raised by Plaintiff: ANSWER SUBJECT TO
   OBJECTION. Adopt the formal-complaints reading; object to "any
   concern" as vague and overbroad, answer as to the January 12
   email and February 3 written complaint.
6. Restructuring account including when each decision was made:
   ANSWER SUBJECT TO OBJECTION ("each decision" vague). NOTE FOR
   COUNSEL, not an objection: the record conflict on the decision
   date must be resolved before any sworn timeline is given.
7. Reason for termination: OBJECTION WITH LIMITED ANSWER. The
   question asks for the reason; the answer states the position
   elimination in the restructuring, one sentence, nothing
   volunteered.
8. Operations managers, ratings, and selection method: ANSWER
   SUBJECT TO OBJECTION. Nonparty employee performance information
   raised; answer given with comparator information limited to what
   selection actually used, protective order reserved.
9. Persons with HOS/ELD knowledge: ANSWER SUBJECT TO OBJECTION
   (overbroad as to "everyone"; answer identifies personnel with
   substantive knowledge).
10. Retention policies and preservation steps: OBJECTION WITH
    LIMITED ANSWER. Litigation hold contents are work product;
    answer describes retention policies and affirms preservation
    steps were taken and when.
11. Insurance: ANSWER.
12. Trial witnesses and persons with knowledge: ANSWER SUBJECT TO
    OBJECTION. Trial witnesses not yet determined (work product as
    to selection); persons with knowledge identified.
