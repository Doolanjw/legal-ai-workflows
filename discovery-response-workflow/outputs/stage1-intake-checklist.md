## Interrogatories

### Interrogatory No. 1

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Identify each person who supplied information used in answering these interrogatories, and state which answers each person supplied information for.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Name the people who helped answer these interrogatories and match each person to the answers they contributed to.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound, two subparts: (a) identify each contributing person (per the definitions, "identify" requires full name, job title, and last known contact information), and (b) map each person to specific answers. The boundary is persons who supplied information for these answers; a careless answer would drag in everyone generally knowledgeable about the case, or counsel's own privileged preparation process.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Internal correspondence gathering answer material; organizational contact records; notes of employee interviews conducted to prepare answers (privilege status for counsel to assess).

---

### Interrogatory No. 2

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Describe the Company's management and reporting structure for its operations department from January 1, 2025 to the present, including each person who held the title of operations manager or an equivalent position.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Explain who reported to whom in the operations department since January 1, 2025, and name everyone who was an operations manager or equivalent during that time.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound, two subparts: (a) the department's management and reporting structure over the stated period, and (b) each person holding the operations manager title or an equivalent position. Bounded to the operations department and to the period January 1, 2025 to the present; a careless answer would describe company-wide structure or reach back before 2025. "Equivalent position" requires a judgment call on which titles qualify.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Organizational charts; job descriptions; personnel rosters; HR records showing title changes and reporting lines.

---

### Interrogatory No. 3

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Identify each person who participated in, was consulted regarding, or approved the decision to terminate Plaintiff's employment, and describe each such person's role in that decision.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Name everyone involved in any way in the decision to fire Plaintiff and say what part each played.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound, two subparts: (a) identify each participant, consultee, or approver, and (b) describe each person's role. The three verbs (participated, consulted, approved) sweep in peripheral involvement, not just the final decisionmaker. Bounded to the termination decision itself; a careless answer would narrate the entire restructuring or Plaintiff's whole employment history, which other requests cover separately.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Termination approval documents; emails and meeting records concerning the decision; HR files on the separation; any decision memoranda.

---

### Interrogatory No. 4

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Describe Plaintiff's performance history during her employment, including each performance evaluation, rating, counseling session, or disciplinary action, the date of each, and the person who administered it.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Give a full account of how Plaintiff performed on the job, itemizing every evaluation, rating, counseling session, and disciplinary action with dates and who administered each.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound: (a) a narrative of performance history, and (b) an itemized list of evaluations, ratings, counseling sessions, and disciplinary actions, each with date and administering person. FLAGGED AS AMBIGUOUS: "including" admits two readings. Reading one, the enumerated items are illustrative and the answer must cover all performance history, formal or informal. Reading two, the enumerated items define the answer and only documented evaluation and discipline events are called for. The chosen reading controls whether informal, undocumented feedback is inside the question. Bounded to Plaintiff's employment period; a careless answer would volunteer characterizations of performance beyond the record.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Performance evaluations; rating records; counseling and disciplinary documentation; supervisor notes; personnel file materials.

### Interrogatory No. 5

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Describe each complaint or concern Plaintiff raised regarding driver hours of service, driver logs, or dispatch scheduling practices, and describe in detail every action the Company took in response to each.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** List every complaint or concern Plaintiff raised about driver hours, logs, or dispatch scheduling, and detail what the Company did about each one.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound, two subparts: (a) each complaint or concern Plaintiff raised on the three listed subjects, and (b) every responsive action the Company took as to each. FLAGGED AS AMBIGUOUS: "complaint or concern" admits two readings. Reading one, formal complaints made through Company channels. Reading two, any expression of concern however informal, including offhand remarks in meetings or messages. The subject matter boundary is the three listed topics (hours of service, driver logs, dispatch scheduling); a careless answer would sweep in complaints Plaintiff raised on unrelated subjects or complaints raised by other employees.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Complaint or incident reports; internal emails and messages raising or responding to the concerns; investigation records; meeting notes; compliance correspondence.

### Interrogatory No. 6

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Explain the restructuring named in the April 6, 2026 memo, covering its origin, timing, business rationale, affected positions, and the timeline of each decision about it.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound, five subparts: (a) who first proposed the restructuring, (b) when it was proposed, (c) the business justification, (d) each position affected, and (e) when each decision concerning it was made. Anchored to the restructuring referenced in the April 6, 2026 memorandum; a careless answer would describe other reorganizations or general business strategy not tied to that memorandum.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** The April 6, 2026 memorandum; restructuring proposals and financial analyses; board or management meeting agendas and minutes; internal communications about the plan; position elimination lists.

### Interrogatory No. 7

**VERBATIM TEXT:** State the reason for the termination of Plaintiff's employment.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Say why Plaintiff was fired.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Single subject, narrow on its face. The boundary is the reason for the termination; a careless answer would argue the case, recite performance history covered by Interrogatory No. 4, or offer multiple shifting rationales without counsel's direction. The answer given here fixes the Company's stated position.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Termination letter or memorandum; separation paperwork; internal documents stating the basis for the decision.

### Interrogatory No. 8

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Identify each person who held an operations manager position at the Company between January 1, 2025 and the present, state each such person's date of hire and most recent performance rating, and describe the criteria used to determine which position or person would be retained in any restructuring.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Name every operations manager since January 1, 2025 with hire date and latest performance rating, and explain how the Company decided who or what position would be kept in any restructuring.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound, three subparts: (a) identify each operations manager in the period, (b) state each one's hire date and most recent performance rating, and (c) describe the retention criteria used in any restructuring. Subparts (a) and (b) are bounded to operations manager positions and the stated period; subpart (c) says "any restructuring," which reaches beyond the April 6, 2026 restructuring if others occurred. A careless answer would produce performance detail beyond the most recent rating or discuss retention decisions for positions outside operations management.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Personnel rosters; hire records; performance ratings for the comparator managers; restructuring selection criteria documents; decision matrices or ranking documents.

### Interrogatory No. 9

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Identify each person with knowledge of the Company's driver hours of service practices, dispatch scheduling practices, or electronic logging device records for the period October 1, 2025 through April 30, 2026.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Name everyone who knows about the Company's driver hours, dispatch scheduling, or electronic logging device records for October 2025 through April 2026.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Single ask with three subject areas (hours of service practices, dispatch scheduling practices, electronic logging device records) bounded to October 1, 2025 through April 30, 2026. "Each person with knowledge" is broad on its face and could literally cover every driver and dispatcher; the boundary question is how far down the roster a complete answer must go. A careless answer would either omit obviously knowledgeable personnel or concede knowledge characterizations better left for counsel.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Personnel rosters for operations, dispatch, and compliance functions; electronic logging device administration records; compliance program documents identifying responsible personnel.

### Interrogatory No. 10

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Describe the Company's document retention policies and each step the Company has taken to preserve documents relating to Plaintiff or her claims since February 3, 2026, including the issuance of any litigation hold.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Describe the Company's document retention policies and everything it has done since February 3, 2026 to preserve documents about Plaintiff or her claims, including any litigation hold.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound, two subparts: (a) the Company's document retention policies, and (b) each preservation step taken since February 3, 2026, including any litigation hold. Subpart (a) has no stated time limit; subpart (b) is bounded by the February 3, 2026 date and by documents relating to Plaintiff or her claims. A careless answer would disclose litigation hold content or counsel communications rather than the fact and timing of preservation steps.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Document retention policy documents; litigation hold notices and distribution records; IT preservation records; custodian acknowledgments.

### Interrogatory No. 11

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Identify each insurance policy that does or may provide coverage for the claims asserted in this action, including the insurer, policy number, policy period, and limits of coverage.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** List every insurance policy that does or might cover the claims in this case, with insurer, policy number, period, and limits.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Single subject with four required data points per policy (insurer, policy number, policy period, coverage limits). "Does or may provide coverage" includes policies where coverage is disputed or reserved. Bounded to the claims asserted in this action; a careless answer would characterize coverage positions or omit policies under a reservation of rights.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Insurance policies (employment practices liability and any other potentially applicable lines); declarations pages; insurer correspondence on coverage.

### Interrogatory No. 12

**VERBATIM TEXT:** Identify each person the Company expects to call as a witness at the trial of this matter, and each person known to the Company to have knowledge of the facts alleged in the Complaint or of any defense raised in the Answer, with a brief description of each person's knowledge.

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Name the Company's expected trial witnesses and everyone the Company knows to have knowledge of the Complaint's allegations or the Answer's defenses, with a short description of what each knows.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound, two distinct groups: (a) persons the Company expects to call at trial, and (b) all persons known to have knowledge of the facts alleged in the Complaint or of any defense in the Answer, each with a brief knowledge description. Group (a) calls for trial strategy that may not yet be set; group (b) overlaps Interrogatory No. 9 but is bounded by the pleadings rather than by subject and date. A careless answer would commit prematurely to a witness list or give knowledge descriptions detailed enough to function as witness statements.

**OBJECTION POSTURE:** ATTORNEY TO SET

**LIKELY EVIDENCE:** Pleadings (Complaint and Answer) as the reference frame; personnel rosters; the witness knowledge already mapped in answering Interrogatories Nos. 3, 5, and 9.

### Request No. 3

**PLAIN RESTATEMENT:** Produce everything about any complaint or concern Plaintiff raised on driver hours, logs, or dispatch, including investigation and response materials.

**SCOPE NOTE:** Compound within one subject: (a) documents and communications concerning the complaints or concerns themselves, and (b) documents concerning any investigation of or response to them. FLAGGED AS AMBIGUOUS: this request says "dispatch practices" where Interrogatory No. 5 says "dispatch scheduling practices," and the wording admits two readings. Reading one, "dispatch practices" is shorthand for the same dispatch scheduling practices as Interrogatory No. 5. Reading two, it is broader and reaches all dispatch operations, not just scheduling. The "complaint or concern" ambiguity flagged under Interrogatory No. 5 (formal complaint versus any informal expression) carries over here as well. A careless response would produce complaint materials from other employees or on other subjects.
