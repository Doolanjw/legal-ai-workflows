## Method notes

1. Coverage is 100 percent of the collected set: 23 documents, CRL-0001 through CRL-0023. Every document has an entry; irrelevant documents are marked NONE with a one line reason.
2. "ROG" means Interrogatory; "RFP" means Request for Production, per the Stage 1 checklist. A document is mapped to an interrogatory when its content supplies material for answering that interrogatory, and to a request for production when the document falls within the request's terms.
3. Derivative requests: ROG No. 1 and RFP No. 1 (documents relied on in answering) and RFP No. 10 (policies identified under ROG No. 11) take their scope from the interrogatory answers, which are not yet drafted. No document is mapped to them at triage; their population is fixed at Stage 4 drafting.
4. No source documents in this set address ROG No. 10 (retention policies and litigation hold), ROG No. 11 (insurance), or RFP No. 10 (insurance policies). That is a collection gap to raise with the client, not a triage conclusion that none exist.
5. ROG No. 12 (trial witnesses and persons with knowledge of the pleadings) is bounded by the Complaint and Answer, which are not in the collected set. The knowledge mapping under ROGs Nos. 3, 5, and 9 below feeds that answer; no separate document calls are made against ROG No. 12.
6. Sensitivity flags follow Protocol 02: any mention of legal advice, in house or outside counsel, settlement, or severance is flagged for the Stage 3 privilege pass. When in doubt, flagged. Triage does not decide privilege.
